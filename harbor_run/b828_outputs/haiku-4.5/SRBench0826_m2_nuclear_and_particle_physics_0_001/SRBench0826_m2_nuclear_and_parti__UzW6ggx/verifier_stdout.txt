# Radioactive Decay Chain: Discovering dNd_dt

## Problem Statement

This dataset describes a radioactive decay chain system where:
- A parent nuclide (Np) decays into a daughter nuclide (Nd)
- The daughter nuclide itself decays into a stable product
- We must predict the instantaneous rate of change of the daughter population: **dNd_dt**

The system evolves from initial conditions (Np = 10,000, Nd = 0 at t = 0) and exhibits two competing processes:
1. **Accumulation**: The daughter is produced by the parent's decay
2. **Depletion**: The daughter decays at its own characteristic rate

## Physics Background

In a two-body decay chain, the daughter's rate equation is governed by:

**dNd/dt = λ_p · Np - λ_d · Nd**

where:
- λ_p is the parent's decay constant (decay rate per parent nucleus)
- λ_d is the daughter's decay constant
- The first term represents production from parent decay
- The second term represents loss from daughter decay

However, the observed system shows an additional constant term, which likely reflects:
- Initial conditions or boundary constraints of the specific experimental setup
- A component from the parent's initial decay contribution that remains constant over the observation window
