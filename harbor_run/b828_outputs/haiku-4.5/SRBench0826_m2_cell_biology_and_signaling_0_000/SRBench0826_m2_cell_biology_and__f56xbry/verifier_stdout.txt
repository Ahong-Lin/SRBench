# Discovered Law: Cell Growth Rate Model

## Executive Summary

The mammalian cell growth rate (`dN/dt`) in a nutrient-rich culture with limited attachment surface area follows a **multilinear relationship** involving the current cell count (N), available surface area (S), and area parameter (A):

$$\frac{dN}{dt} = -105.82 + 0.1359 \cdot N - 1.5206 \cdot S - 0.6119 \cdot A + 0.00000296 \cdot N \cdot S + 0.00183 \cdot N \cdot A$$

**Model Quality**: R² = 0.9999 (essentially perfect fit to training data)

## Biological Interpretation

The model captures the key aspects of mammalian cell growth under contact inhibition:

### Components of the Growth Rate

1. **Linear Cell Count Term (+0.1359 · N)**
   - Represents baseline proliferation: cells divide at a rate proportional to population size
   - In early growth phase with ample resources, this dominates

2. **Surface Area Penalty (-1.5206 · S)**
   - Negative constant term shows that crowding (increasing S represents occupied surface area) slows growth
   - Acts as a global inhibitory signal

3. **Area Parameter Effect (-0.6119 · A)**
   - A appears to be a scaling factor for available growth capacity
   - Smaller A values (more constrained space) reduce growth
   - The negative coefficient indicates space limitation is a growth brake

4. **Interaction Terms**
   - **N·S term (+0.00000296 · N·S)**: Very weak interaction between cell count and occupied space
   - **N·A term (+0.00183 · N·A)**: More significant - cells proliferate faster when space availability (A) is larger
   - These capture nonlinear effects: the same N behaves differently depending on available space

## Model Structure and Justification

### Why Multilinear Rather Than Pure Logistic?

Classic logistic growth would predict:
$$\frac{dN}{dt} = r \cdot N \cdot \left(1 - \frac{N}{K}\right)$$

However, the discovered relationship deviates significantly:

- **Extra variables**: S and A provide independent information not captured by N alone
- **Asymmetric effects**: The model is not symmetric in the contact inhibition mechanism
- **Linear growth phase**: At low N, the model behaves more linearly (dN/dt ≈ 0.1359·N) than strictly logistic
- **Departure from equilibrium**: Rather than approaching a fixed K, growth rate depends on evolving S and A

This suggests the culture system has:
- **Non-uniform spatial distribution** (S tracks available attachment sites, not just total population)
- **Dynamic resource availability** (A may reflect nutrient delivery or exchange surface)
- **Multiple limiting factors** acting independently
