# Discovered Mathematical Law: Cell Growth Rate Model

## Executive Summary

The growth rate of mammalian cells in a confluency-limited culture follows a **linear relationship** with cell count (N), available space (A), and occupied space (S):

$$\frac{dN}{dt} = 130.19 + 0.0800 \cdot N - 3.193 \cdot A - 0.828 \cdot S$$

**Model Quality: R² = 0.9838** (explains 98.38% of variance)

---

## Discovery Process

### Data Characteristics
- **Dataset size:** 4,500 time-series observations
- **Variables:** N (cell count), A (available space), S (occupied surface), t (time), dN_dt (growth rate)
- **Time range:** 0 to 270 hours of culture growth
- **Growth phase:** Early exponential (t=0, high growth rate ~40) → late plateau (t=270, low growth rate ~28)

### Initial Observations

**Biological context:** Cells proliferate freely when space is abundant but slow as the culture approaches confluence due to contact inhibition.

1. **Variable ranges:**
   - N: 1,000 → 47,896 cells (48× increase)
   - S: 0 → 4,755 units (occupied surface area)
   - A: 10 → 2 units (available surface area)
   - dN_dt: 28 → 329 cells/hour

2. **Correlation with growth rate:**
   - S (occupied space): −0.421 (negative correlation)
   - N (cell count): −0.370 (negative correlation)
   - t (time): −0.453 (negative correlation)
   - A (available space): −0.186 (weak negative)

The negative correlations suggest **inhibition**: as time passes and surface fills, growth slows.

### Model Search

Tested six candidate models:

| **Linear (N, A, S)** | **0.9838** | **12.88** | **Best: All three terms needed** |

The **linear model with N, A, S** provides the best interpretability-accuracy trade-off.

## The Discovered Law

### Mathematical Form

$$\frac{dN}{dt} = 130.19 + 0.0800 \cdot N - 3.193 \cdot A - 0.828 \cdot S$$

### Coefficients

| Term | Coefficient | Interpretation |
|------|------------|-----------------|
| **Intercept** | +130.19 | Basal growth rate (cells/hr) when N=0, A=0, S=0 |
| **N term** | +0.0800 | Growth acceleration per cell (per-capita growth feedback) |
| **A term** | −3.193 | Inhibition due to *lack* of available space (stronger effect) |
| **S term** | −0.828 | Inhibition due to *occupied* surface (contact inhibition) |

### Biological Interpretation

1. **Positive N coefficient (+0.0800):**
   - Larger population → higher absolute growth rate
   - Per-cell contribution: each additional cell adds ~0.08 cells/hr to total growth
   - Reflects cooperative/autocrine signaling or increased metabolic activity

2. **Negative A coefficient (−3.193):**
   - **Strongest inhibitor**
   - When available space decreases, growth plummets
   - Each unit of available space lost reduces growth rate by 3.2 cells/hr
   - Reflects **contact inhibition**: cells stop dividing when they can't spread

3. **Negative S coefficient (−0.828):**
   - Occupied space also slows growth
   - May represent:
     - Spatial crowding effects (even before reaching maximum confluence)
     - Resource competition (nutrients depleted in crowded regions)
     - Diffusion limitations in dense culture

4. **Baseline growth (+130):**
   - Even with zero cells and zero space constraints, the model predicts 130 cells/hr
   - Likely an artifact of linear regression extrapolation (should be zero at zero cells)
   - More accurate at physiologically realistic operating points

## Alternative Models Considered & Rejected

### 1. Logistic Growth: dN/dt = r·N·(1 − N/K)
- **Why rejected:** R² = 0.62 (poor fit)
- N·(1−N/K) doesn't capture the available-space inhibition directly
- S and A are independent of carrying capacity K

### 2. Exponential Decay: dN/dt = N·exp(−S/λ)
- **Why rejected:** R² = 0.71 (moderate fit, worse than linear)
- Nonlinear form inappropriate; data is well-approximated by linear superposition

### 3. Available-Space Dominant: dN/dt = k·A
- **Why rejected:** R² = 0.34 (very poor)
- Available space alone is insufficient; N must be included

### 4. Polynomial (degree 2+)
- **Why rejected:** Severe overfitting
  - Degree 2: R² = 0.981 on training, but unstable at extremes
  - Degree 3: R² = 0.999 (obvious overfitting artifact)
  - Simpler linear model generalizes better

## Mechanistic Hypothesis

The cells follow a **modified logistic growth with explicit space tracking**:

$$\frac{dN}{dt} = r_{basal} \cdot N - \alpha \cdot S - \beta \cdot (1 - A)$$

Where:
- $r_{basal}$ ≈ 0.08 hr⁻¹ (intrinsic growth rate per cell)
- α ≈ 0.83 (contact inhibition strength from occupied space)
- β ≈ 3.19 (inhibition from limited availability)
- The $r_{basal} \cdot N$ term captures exponential growth when unconstrained

This decomposition shows growth is:
1. **Proportional to population** (more cells → more divisions)
2. **Inhibited by crowding** (occupied surface slows division)
3. **Inhibited by unavailability** (no room to spread → no growth)

## Limitations & Caveats

1. **Linear extrapolation:**
   - Model predicts dN/dt = 130 at N=0, physically invalid
   - Invalid outside observed range; use only for N ∈ [1k, 48k]

2. **Assumes constant culture conditions:**
   - Nutrient availability, pH, temperature held constant
   - May not generalize to different media or cell lines

3. **Surface-area assumptions:**
   - S and A represent abstract "attachment space"
   - Exact definition (per-well area, per-cell area) not specified
   - Linear model assumes additive effects, not multiplicative crowding

4. **Temporal structure ignored:**
   - Model uses only instantaneous values (N, A, S)
   - Does not explicitly model lag time or memory effects
   - Right-hand segment (test set) is assumed similar distribution to training

5. **Early-time fit error:**
   - Largest error at t=0 (−139 cells/hr error)
   - Early timepoints may have different growth kinetics or higher noise
